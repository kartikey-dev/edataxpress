# Project brief

## Business

- Brand: eDataXpress
- Owner/contact person supplied by requester: Ayan (LinkedIn profile supplied as `dmayan`)
- Operating location: Delhi, India
- Positioning: B2B business portfolio / solutions provider
- Brand tagline visible in supplied logo: “Connecting Businesses Globally”
- Service pillars: Publication, Printing Solutions, Digital Studio Setup, Digital Marketing

## Primary objective

Turn the existing demo into a scalable, high-performing, premium business website that explains the company, presents its services and relevant products, builds trust through verifiable evidence, and generates qualified enquiries.

## Audiences

- Schools, colleges, universities, training institutes, and education decision-makers (especially for Digital Studio Setup).
- Businesses, institutions, publishers, and organizations requiring printing/publication solutions.
- Organizations seeking digital marketing and online presence services.
- Procurement, administration, marketing, and leadership stakeholders evaluating vendors.

These audience groupings are strategic assumptions based on the service categories; confirm with Ayan.

## Primary conversion

A qualified enquiry: request a quote, request a product demonstration, or discuss a project. Avoid checkout/cart flows unless the owner explicitly wants e-commerce.

## Success measures

- Qualified enquiries and enquiry conversion rate.
- Click-through rate to contact / WhatsApp / email (once confirmed).
- Organic impressions and non-branded search clicks by service landing page.
- Core Web Vitals and mobile performance.
- Form completion and error rate.
- Accessibility and Sonar quality gates.
