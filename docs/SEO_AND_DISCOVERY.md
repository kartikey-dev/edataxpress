# SEO, local discovery, and AI answer readiness

## Technical SEO

- Unique metadata per route.
- Canonical domain confirmed before launch.
- Sitemap generated from published routes.
- robots.txt allows public pages and blocks private/staging paths.
- Open Graph/Twitter metadata with optimized share image.
- Breadcrumbs and descriptive internal anchors.
- Correct 301 redirects for renamed routes.
- Custom 404 page.
- No duplicate indexable query/filter URLs.

## Structured data

Use JSON-LD only where accurate:

- `Organization` / appropriate `LocalBusiness` subtype for business identity.
- `Service` for individual service pages where useful.
- `Product` only if a real product is represented and the required fields are accurate.
- `BreadcrumbList` for nested routes.

Do not invent ratings, offers, price, availability, founding year, address, or telephone.

## Delhi/local SEO

- Confirm official NAP (name, address, phone) with Ayan.
- Ensure website and public profiles use consistent details.
- Use natural Delhi/India location references where relevant; do not keyword-stuff.
- Create useful pages for real service areas only if the business serves them.
- Add a map only after the exact public business address is confirmed.

## GEO/AEO

- Answer real buyer questions directly in page copy.
- Use descriptive headings and concise explanations.
- Include clear product model names and verified specifications.
- Keep a concise `llms.txt` aligned with public pages.
- Ensure content is useful to humans first; do not treat AI-specific files as a ranking shortcut.

## Measurement

- Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
- Analytics choice and consent reviewed with owner.
- Track enquiry submit, phone click, email click, WhatsApp click, datasheet download, and product demo request.
- Avoid collecting unnecessary personal data.
